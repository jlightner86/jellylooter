Use a VPN

Send JellyLooter's internet traffic through your VPN. Your LAN (local Jellyfin, Sonarr/Radarr, this page) stays direct.

Provider

Region

Pick the closest region for the best speed

WireGuard configuration

Paste the .conf contents or upload the file

Kill switch

On: if the VPN drops, all internet traffic is blocked and downloads wait until it reconnects. Off: if the VPN fails, downloads keep going without the VPN.

🔒 Using the kill switch is more secure - it keeps your real IP address hidden even if the VPN connection fails.

LAN subnets

Addresses that never go through the VPN (your home network)
